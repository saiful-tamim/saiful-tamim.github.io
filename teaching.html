Teaching

I have taught as an instructor of record in the Department of Mathematics at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, across a range spanning pre-calculus through differential equations and mathematics for data science. My teaching emphasizes building physical and geometric intuition alongside analytical technique, so that students can recognize where the mathematics they are learning actually appears in the world.
